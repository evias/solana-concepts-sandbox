


| Scope | Mandatory input | Likely token type | SAS role | Token-2022 role |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Real estate | title, parcel ID, jurisdiction, ownership proof, legal structure, appraisal | fungible shares or NFT | prove title, KYC, lien status, document hashes | metadata, transfer hook, restrictions |
| Ownership contracts / vehicle | VIN, title, registration, contract terms, no lien proof | NFT or fractional token | prove title, ownership, contract validity | metadata, transfer hook, compliance |
| Collectibles | authenticity, provenance, grading, custody, media proof | NFT, or fractional token | prove authenticity and ownership | metadata, redemption logic, restrictions |
| Insurance contracts | policyholder, policy terms, coverage, asset, proof of issuance | non-transferable credential or risk token | prove policy issuance and claim status | non-transferable, metadata, hooks |
